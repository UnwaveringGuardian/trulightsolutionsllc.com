About TruLight Solutions LLC

TruLight Solutions LLC is a telecommunications construction contractor specializing in aerial fiber optic installation, strand construction, utility pole make-ready, and outside plant (OSP) construction. We support telecommunications infrastructure projects throughout the East Coast and are available to mobilize crews nationwide.

Who We Are

TruLight Solutions LLC was built around a commitment to dependable field execution, safety-focused operations, and straightforward communication. Our work focuses on aerial telecommunications construction, including fiber deployment, strand installation, and utility pole make-ready.

We support infrastructure projects across multiple states and work with telecommunications construction companies and prime contractors. We also welcome opportunities to collaborate directly with telecommunications carriers and network operators.

Our goal is to provide reliable construction support, maintain professional standards, and build lasting relationships with our project partners.

Our Field Operations & Capabilities
