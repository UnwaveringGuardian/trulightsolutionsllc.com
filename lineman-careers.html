Full Job Description

TruLight Solutions LLC is seeking experienced Aerial Fiber Lineman to join our growing team. We are looking for dependable crew leads with telecommunications construction experience who take pride in producing safe, high-quality work.

This position is responsible for the aerial installation of fiber optic infrastructure, including strand construction, fiber placement, make-ready work, and related outside plant (OSP) construction activities. The selected Lineman will be responsible for leading a two-person crew (Lineman and Helper).

TruLight Solutions provides the bucket truck and trailer. We are looking for Lineman who are motivated, safety-focused, and capable of completing work efficiently with minimal supervision.

Responsibilities

  • Install strand, fiber optic cable, and associated aerial hardware.
  • Perform make-ready work, and other aerial construction tasks.
  • Install and relocate communication cables in accordance with construction maps and specifications.
  • Read and interpret engineering maps and work packages.
  • Operate bucket trucks safely and efficiently.
  • Ensure all work meets customer specifications, quality standards, and safety requirements.
  • Maintain a clean and organized job site.
  • Communicate effectively with field supervisors regarding production, material needs, and project updates.
  • Follow all OSHA, company, and customer safety requirements.

Qualifications Required

  • Minimum 2 years of aerial telecommunications construction experience.
  • Experience with strand construction, fiber lashing, and aerial fiber installation.
  • Experience performing make-ready work.
  • Ability to read and interpret construction maps.
  • Experience operating bucket trucks.
  • Valid driver's license with a clean driving record.
  • Ability to work outdoors in all weather conditions.
  • Ability to lift and carry 75 pounds.
  • Strong work ethic with attention to safety and quality.

Preferred

  • Experience working on Verizon, Frontier, AT&T, Fidium, Spectrum, GoNetSpeed, or similar telecommunications projects.
  • OSHA 10 or OSHA 30 certification is a plus.

Compensation

Base pay of $38.46–$43.27 per hour, equivalent to approximately $80,000–$90,000 annually based on a 40-hour workweek, depending on experience and qualifications.

Overtime compensation is additional and paid in accordance with applicable wage and hour laws.

Travel, Lodging & Per Diem

This is a traveling position. TruLight Solutions LLC performs telecommunications construction projects across multiple states. Employees must be willing to travel to assigned project locations and remain away from home for extended periods as needed. TruLight supports employees working away from home by providing:

  • Company-paid lodging.
  • Company-paid travel to and from project assignments.
  • Daily meal per diem provided while traveling for work.
  • Company-paid travel home every third weekend while on an extended assignment.

Travel arrangements and applicable per diem details will be reviewed during the hiring process.
