Aerial Fiber Lineman / Foreman Crew Lead

$38.46–$43.27/hour · Approx. $80,000–$90,000/year

Lead a two-person aerial construction crew installing fiber optic infrastructure, strand, and make-ready work. Minimum two years of aerial telecommunications construction experience required. Traveling position with current projects in Maine.

Apply Now

Full Job Description

TruLight Solutions LLC is seeking experienced Aerial Fiber Lineman to join our growing team. We are looking for dependable crew leads with telecommunications construction experience who take pride in producing safe, high-quality work.

This position is responsible for the aerial installation of fiber optic infrastructure, including strand construction, fiber placement, make-ready work, and related outside plant (OSP) construction activities. The selected Lineman will be responsible for leading a two-person crew (Lineman and Helper).

TruLight Solutions provides the bucket truck and trailer. We are looking for Lineman who are motivated, safety-focused, and capable of completing work efficiently with minimal supervision.

Responsibilities

  • Install strand, fiber optic cable, and associated aerial hardware.
  • Perform make-ready work, and other aerial construction tasks.
  • Install and relocate communication cables in accordance with construction maps and specifications.
  • Read and interpret engineering maps and work packages.
  • Operate bucket trucks safely and efficiently.
  • Ensure all work meets customer specifications, quality standards, and safety requirements.
  • Maintain a clean and organized job site.
  • Communicate effectively with field supervisors regarding production, material needs, and project updates.
  • Follow all OSHA, company, and customer safety requirements.

Qualifications Required

  • Minimum 2 years of aerial telecommunications construction experience.
  • Experience with strand construction, fiber lashing, and aerial fiber installation.
  • Experience performing make-ready work.
  • Ability to read and interpret construction maps.
  • Experience operating bucket trucks.
  • Valid driver's license with a clean driving record.
  • Ability to work outdoors in all weather conditions.
  • Ability to lift and carry 75 pounds.
  • Strong work ethic with attention to safety and quality.

Preferred

  • Experience working on Verizon, Frontier, AT&T, Fidium, Spectrum, GoNetSpeed, or similar telecommunications projects.
  • OSHA 10 or OSHA 30 certification is a plus.

Compensation

Base pay of $38.46–$43.27 per hour, equivalent to approximately $80,000–$90,000 annually based on a 40-hour workweek, depending on experience and qualifications.

Overtime compensation is additional and paid in accordance with applicable wage and hour laws.

Travel, Lodging & Per Diem

This is a traveling position, with current projects located in Maine. TruLight supports employees working away from home by providing:

  • Company-paid lodging.
  • Company-paid travel to and from project assignments.
  • Daily meal per diem provided while traveling for work.
  • Company-paid travel home every third weekend while on an extended assignment.

Aerial Fiber Helper

$31.25–$36.06/hour · Approx. $65,000–$75,000/year

Support an aerial fiber lineman with material handling, fiber and strand installation, make-ready work, and jobsite safety. Prior telecommunications experience preferred but not necessarily required. High-travel position with current projects in Maine.

Apply Now

Full Job Description

TruLight Solutions LLC is seeking a dependable and motivated Aerial Fiber Helper to join our growing telecommunications construction team.

The Aerial Fiber Helper works as part of a two-person aerial construction crew alongside an experienced Aerial Fiber Lineman / Foreman Crew Lead. This position assists with the installation of aerial fiber optic infrastructure, including strand construction, fiber placement, make-ready work, and related outside plant (OSP) construction activities.

The Helper plays an important role in crew safety, productivity, material handling, traffic and work-zone safety, and supporting the Lineman during aerial construction operations.

This is a high-travel position. Employees must be willing and able to travel to project locations as required and may remain away from home for extended periods.

TruLight provides the bucket truck, trailer, tools, safety equipment, signage, and project materials necessary to perform assigned work.

Responsibilities

  • Assist the Lineman / Foreman with installation of strand, fiber optic cable, and associated aerial hardware.
  • Assist with fiber lashing, cable placement, make-ready work, and other aerial construction activities.
  • Prepare, organize, load, unload, and stage tools, materials, and equipment for daily construction activities.
  • Assist with setting up and maintaining safe work zones, including placement of cones, signs, and other required traffic-control or safety equipment.
  • Perform ground-level construction activities in support of aerial operations.
  • Handle and feed strand, fiber, cable, and other materials as directed by the Lineman / Foreman.
  • Assist with installing and relocating communication cables in accordance with construction maps, specifications, and crew instructions.
  • Learn to read and understand construction maps and work packages.
  • Maintain a clean, organized, and safe job site, bucket truck, trailer, and work area.
  • Inspect and properly care for assigned tools, equipment, and safety gear.
  • Communicate effectively with the Lineman / Foreman and field supervisors regarding materials, work activities, safety concerns, and project needs.
  • Follow instructions from the Lineman / Foreman and other TruLight supervisors.
  • Follow all applicable OSHA, TruLight, and customer safety requirements.
  • Perform other reasonable duties associated with aerial telecommunications construction as assigned.

Qualifications Required

  • Valid driver's license with an acceptable driving record.
  • Ability and willingness to travel extensively for project assignments.
  • Ability to work outdoors in varying weather conditions.
  • Ability to lift and carry up to 75 pounds.
  • Ability to perform physically demanding work, including lifting, carrying, pulling, bending, climbing, and working on uneven terrain.
  • Ability to safely use hand tools, construction equipment, and personal protective equipment.
  • Ability to follow instructions and work effectively as part of a two-person crew.
  • Dependable attendance and willingness to report to assigned crew meet-up points and project locations as required.
  • Strong work ethic with attention to safety, quality, and productivity.
  • Ability to communicate effectively with crew members and supervisors.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Previous telecommunications, utility, construction, or outside plant (OSP) experience.
  • Previous aerial fiber construction experience.
  • Familiarity with strand construction, fiber lashing, make-ready work, or aerial cable installation.
  • Experience working around bucket trucks and aerial construction equipment.
  • Experience working on Verizon, Frontier, AT&T, Brightspeed, Spectrum, GoNetSpeed, or similar telecommunications projects.
  • OSHA 10 or OSHA 30 certification is a plus.

Previous telecommunications experience is preferred but may not be required for an otherwise qualified candidate who demonstrates the ability and willingness to learn.

Compensation

Base pay of $31.25–$36.06 per hour, equivalent to approximately $65,000–$75,000 annually based on a 40-hour workweek, depending on experience and qualifications.

Overtime compensation is additional and paid in accordance with applicable wage and hour laws.
